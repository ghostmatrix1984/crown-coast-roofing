# Image credits — Crown & Coast Roofing (demo)

All photography on this site is sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Images are
resized for the web; several are also cropped to frame the subject and to
remove third-party signage from view (hero, project-reroof-after,
project-chimney-before, project-tiles-after), and service-flat-roof is trimmed
to remove a ladder and an embossed manufacturer mark. The same credits are
rendered in the site footer. These images illustrate a **fictional
demonstration business**; none of them shows a real Crown & Coast Roofing
project or customer. Replace with owned or licensed client photography before
any commercial launch.
